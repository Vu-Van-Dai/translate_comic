The user (2026-10-14) noted BL romance involving under-18 characters is inherent to many series, and future series will include mature/explicit content.

Agreed scope:
- **Translate faithfully**, including non-sexual romance between characters under 18 as the story tells it. Don't soften or skip it.
- **Explicit content between adults:** translate normally.
- **Sexual content involving characters under 18:** never translate it or put it in any output. List the affected pages for the user to handle and continue with the rest of the chapter.
- **Videos:** cute, funny or family moments with child characters are fine. No romantic or "couple" promotional edits centred on a character who is a minor in that part of the story. Romantic edits are fine once both characters are adults.

**Why:** the user accepts the romance as part of the source. I hold the line only on sexualising minors, and on promotional romantic framing of children.
**How to apply:** flag affected pages briefly, without lecturing, and keep working. See [[video-feature]].
